# BRIEF · meu-troco-home

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Papel claro, corte seco, verde do monograma, voz direta.
   References: extrato de papel, caderno fechado, uma pergunta dita em voz alta.

2. **Scroll journey.** A pergunta do salário → o método abandonado → a virada
   (“não é disciplina”) → o mês legível → a conta.

3. **Energy curve.** Quiet on the title page, colder on the problem, one short
   sentence on the turn, the largest room on the month, a close that holds.

4. **Feeling curve + peak.** See below. Peak lives in the month chapter.

5. **Signature seed.** The margin is a bank statement that gets stamped as you
   scroll and doubles as the index.

6. **Aesthetic range.** Editorial (paper, folios, measure, restraint). Not
   premium-minimal dark with one accent.

7. **World structure.** Distinct scenes. Hard cuts between grounds. Not one
   unbroken world.

8. **Assets on hand.** Monogram (`public/new_rebrand/nobg.png`), brand green
   (`public/new_rebrand/bg.jpg`). No product photography. No KIE key at build
   time → paper planes in CSS, photoreal plates deferred.
